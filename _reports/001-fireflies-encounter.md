# Fireflies Encounter

**Subject:** Encounter with **Vinnie**, **Two Unidentified Fireflies** and **“Big Mac”**  
**Location:** Pusta

**Reporting Individual:** Tybo  
**Classification:** Verified Intelligence

**Source Reliability:** Trustworthy

## 1. Summary of Encounter

While travelling through **Pusta** to visit the **well** I was approached by **Vinnie** and **two unidentified Fireflies**. An individual known as **“Big Mac”** was also present and identified himself as a **journalist**.

To hide my connection to **TL** I used a **false alias** and a fake backstory. This allowed me to lower their guard and gather information about **TL** and the **Helping Hands raid**.

## 2. Intelligence on **TL**

When asked about **TL's reputation** Vinnie stated:

> “They claim to be good and friendly, but their actions say otherwise.”

Vinnie alleged that **TL lacks loyal members** and forces anyone capable of fighting to join its ranks. He contrasted this with the **Fireflies** who he claimed never forces people to fight.

**Vinnie advised me to stay away from TL.** He described the group as **dangerous and untrustworthy**.

**Assessment:** Vinnie portrayed **TL** as an organisation that relies on **coercion rather than loyalty** and therefore advises lone/new travelers to stay away from TL.

## 3. Intelligence on the **Helping Hands Raid**

When questioned about the **Helping Hands raid** Vinnie stated:

> “I can confirm with 100% certainty that TL was behind it. They did it for a bogus, stupid reason.”

Vinnie confirmed that the **Fireflies condemn the raid** and remain on good terms with **Helping Hands**.

He explained:

> “We have never seen Helping Hands do anything bad. So we remain on good terms with them.”

## 4. Opinions on **TL's Strength and Reputation**

During the conversation I raised the accusation that some people consider **TL a dictatorial or terrorist organisation**.

Vinnie responded:

> “That sums it up.”

**Big Mac**, who identified himself as a **journalist** also commented on **TL's military strength**. He claimed that **raiding TL is surprisingly easy** and that the group presents itself as powerful while being weaker in practice.

**Assessment:** Both individuals expressed negative views of **TL**. Vinnie's statements focused on the group's conduct and ideology while **Big Mac questioned its actual strength**.

## 5. Key Findings

- **Reputation:** Vinnie claims that **TL's actions contradict its friendly public image**.
- **Recruitment:** Vinnie alleges that **TL forces people to fight** due to a lack of loyal members.
- **Helping Hands raid:** Vinnie holds **TL responsible** and states that the Fireflies condemn the attack.
- **Fireflies' position:** The group reportedly maintains **good relations with Helping Hands**.
- **Military strength:** **Big Mac claims TL is weaker than it appears** and that raids against the group are relatively easy.
- **Political hostility:** Vinnie agreed with the characterisation of **TL as dictatorial or terrorist**.
- **Other individuals:** Two unidentified **Fireflies** were present during the encounter.

## 6. Conclusion

The encounter revealed significant **hostility between the Fireflies and TL**. Vinnie accused **TL** of coercive recruitment and responsibility for the **Helping Hands raid**. He also expressed support for Helping Hands. Meanwhile **Big Mac questioned TL's actual military strength** claiming its reputation exceeds its capabilities.

These statements provide useful insight into how members of other groups perceive **TL**.
